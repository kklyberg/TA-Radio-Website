Voice Channel Capacity Comparison

Analog vs TDMA vs FDMA — How many simultaneous conversations fit in the same spectrum
Analog FM (1 channel)
TDMA – DMR (2 slots)
FDMA – NXDN / dPMR (2 × 6.25 kHz)
Key takeaway: In a standard 12.5 kHz channel, classic Analog carries 1 voice path. Both TDMA (DMR) and FDMA (NXDN) deliver 2 simultaneous voice paths from the same RF resource, effectively doubling capacity without needing extra frequencies.
