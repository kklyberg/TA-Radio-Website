What Is Push-to-Talk over Cellular?

Push-to-Talk over Cellular (PoC) delivers instant group communication over 4G/LTE, 5G, and Wi-Fi networks instead of traditional radio towers and repeaters. Users press a single button and talk to individuals or entire groups across a facility, city, or nationwide.

How PoC Works

PoC devices connect to a cloud server through cellular data or Wi-Fi. When the PTT button is pressed, voice is routed instantly through the cloud to the selected talk group or individual — no repeaters or FCC licenses required for wide-area coverage.
