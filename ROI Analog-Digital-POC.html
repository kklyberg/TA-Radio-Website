Simple decision guide for customers:
• Analog — Lowest cost if you only need a small area and few users.
• Digital (DMR/NXDN) — Best long-term value when you need more capacity, better audio, and room to grow on licensed channels.
• PoC — Often the highest ROI when coverage must span cities, states, or the whole country without building radio infrastructure.
