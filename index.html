Digital Migration & PoC Enterprise Hub

Transition legacy analog fleets to high-performance Digital LMR (DMR/NXDN), or deploy instant nationwide Push-to-Talk over Cellular (PoC) infrastructure across carrier networks.

Analog → Digital LMR Path

Migrating from legacy analog to narrow-band Digital LMR doubles operational channel capacity on your current frequencies using advanced TDMA/FDMA technology. Digital processing eliminates fringe static, filters out background commercial noise, and enables AES encryption protocols. Dual-mode base stations and portables ensure seamless interoperability throughout your phased upgrade lifecycle.

Digital → PoC & RoIP Gateways

Push-to-Talk over Cellular (PoC) digitizes enterprise voice traffic into secure IP packets routed across nationwide 4G LTE/5G commercial grids and private Wi-Fi networks. This deployment pattern drops local repeater tower footprints, frequency coordination licensing, and physical sight limits completely. Integrating a hardware Radio over IP (RoIP) gateway allows you to bridge active field radios instantly into wide-area cellular talkgroups.
