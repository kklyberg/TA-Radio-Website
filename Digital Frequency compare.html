Analog vs TDMA vs FDMA

How the RF channel is used — Waveform & Spectrum View
Analog FM 1 Voice Path
TDMA (DMR style) 2 Voice Paths – Time Slots
FDMA (NXDN / 6.25 kHz) 2 Voice Paths – Frequency Split
How to read the diagrams:
• Analog – continuous signal occupies the full channel the entire time (one conversation).
• TDMA – same frequency is shared by taking turns in short time slots (Slot 1 / Slot 2).
• FDMA – the 12.5 kHz channel is split into two narrower 6.25 kHz channels that can be used at the same time.
